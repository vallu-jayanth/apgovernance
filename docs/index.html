What do these words mean?
Crore / lakh
1 lakh = ₹1,00,000. 1 crore = 100 lakh = ₹1,00,00,000. 1 lakh crore = ₹1,00,000 crore.
Capital spending
Money spent on things that last: roads, buildings, irrigation, schools. It builds the state's future.
Revenue spending
Day-to-day running costs: salaries, pensions, interest, subsidies, electricity.
Revenue deficit
How much more the state spent on running costs than it earned. It is covered by borrowing.
Voucher
The record of one payment. Each should have a bill and supporting papers behind it.
Abstract Contingent bill
A request to draw cash in advance. A detailed bill with receipts must follow within 90 days.
Utilisation certificate
A document showing how a grant was spent, sent back to whoever gave the grant.
CAG
The Comptroller and Auditor General of India, the constitutional auditor of all government spending.

How payments are flagged
Red flags
One payment larger than the work's estimate; total paid above the work's approved cost; the same amount paid twice for the same work; a voucher number used twice; a cash payment; a photo-proof problem on the matched work.
Worth asking
No photo-tagged work matches a works payment; no clear description; an amount just under ₹1, 2, 3, 5, 10, 20 or 50 lakh (bills split to avoid approval or tender limits); three or more payments of the same amount; a payment dated on a Sunday.
For information
Large round amounts; payments in the last week of March.
Photo proof problems
No GPS location; tagged outside Andhra Pradesh; a "completed" photo uploaded a month or more before the foundation or roof photo of the same work; the same photo used for different works; tagged more than 15 km from the panchayat's other works; three or more different works photographed at exactly the same point.
Limits
The cash book has no bills or vendor names, and photos cannot be checked for being blank from here. Matching a payment to a photo is by the work's name and can miss or mismatch. Treat every flag as a question to ask.
Take action

Ask questions, get the papers

At the Gram Sabha

Every gram panchayat must hold Gram Sabha meetings. Bring the flagged vouchers and ask the Sarpanch and Panchayat Secretary to show the bills and the work.

File an RTI request

Write to the Public Information Officer at the Gram Panchayat or Mandal Parishad office. Ask for copies of the bills, measurement book pages, work photos and payment proof for each voucher number. A reply is due within 30 days.

Visit the work

Use Satellite view and the photo link on each work to find it. Check that it exists, matches the description and looks like the money spent.
